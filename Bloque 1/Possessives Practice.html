👈 Adjectives vs Pronouns 👉

My, Your, His, Her... Van antes de la cosa.
Ej: It is my 🚗.
Mine, Yours, His, Hers... Van solos (al final).
Ej: The 🚗 is mine.

✍️ Ejercicio 2: ¿Adjetivo o Pronombre?

my / mine your / yours his / his her / hers our / ours their / theirs
Ejemplos:
1. This is my book 📘. (Va antes del libro)
2. The book 📘 is mine. (Va solo al final)
